One Place for All of It

When we started, DollhouseMCP had a blog and Dollhouse Research had a blog, and they really didn’t know about each other. That was fine at the time, when we only had two projects. But, we have more than two projects now, and we’ll have several more coming.

So we’re reorganizing things. The Dollhouse Research blog is now the hub. Every post from every project will show up here, tagged with the project it belongs to. And, each project site will still keep its own blog page showing just its own posts. If you follow one project, you can read it there. If you want the whole picture, come here.

Nothing’s moved, and no links are broken. The posts live where they always did… this page just finally knows about all the other pages.

Here’s how it works going forward. DollhouseMCP posts publish on dollhousemcp.com and get indexed here the same day. Projects that don’t have their own blog yet, like MCP-AQL, AILIS, and Elemental Surveys, publish right here under their project tag. When one of them grows to the point it needs its own blog, the posts keep their links, and the index keeps pointing at them.

And, we now finally have an RSS feed for this site, so you can follow everything in one subscription.

Hope that makes it easy for folks. We’ll have more coming, particularly on the research side.
