Dollhouse Research builds open-source infrastructure for AI agents, and published its first peer-reviewed paper at MAS-GAIN 2026.  
Dollhouse Research builds applied AI systems. The portfolio spans core platform development, community distribution, protocol design, integration runtimes, and architecture research. Current priorities include DollhouseMCP, the Dollhouse Collection, MCP-AQL, Bridge, AILIS, and supporting tooling such as Merview. The objective is practical: ship systems that are technically strong, understandable to builders, and useful in real workflows.
- **October 8, 2026:** First peer-reviewed paper published at MAS-GAIN 2026: [Sufficiently Advanced Agents Are Indistinguishable from Erlang](/research/sufficiently-advanced-agents/).
