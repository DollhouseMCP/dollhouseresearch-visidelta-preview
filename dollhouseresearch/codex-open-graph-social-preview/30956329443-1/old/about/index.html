About Dollhouse Research

Dollhouse Research is the home for products, protocols, and applied AI systems work from DollhouseMCP Inc.

The legal entity is DollhouseMCP Inc, operating as DBA Dollhouse Research.

Mission

Build practical AI infrastructure that is understandable, composable, and useful in real production workflows.

That means combining platform delivery, protocol design, integration tooling, and research exploration while keeping the work accessible to builders.

Founder and Leadership

Mick Darling is the CEO and Founder of DollhouseMCP Inc, and the creator of DollhouseMCP.

He has led long-running work in natural language processing, holds multiple NLP-related patents, and founded Tomorrowish.

Mick has applied that NLP expertise to Media/Tech products that reached millions of viewers through Tomorrowish and other companies he has founded, with nearly two decades of experience across Product, PMM, Business Development, and Strategy.

Contributors to Dollhouse Research also bring deep protocol-development and NLP experience, including patent-backed work, and remain active in AI tooling and model training.

He is based in Massachusetts.
